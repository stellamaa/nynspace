My name is Stella and I run Nyn Space, a meditation and experimental mindfulness practise that focuses on creativity and self-realisation. As a certified Yoga Nidra Teacher, I offer guided meditation sessions anywhere you can lay comfortably; in the work place, art studios, festivals, most places can be transformed into a space of presence and groundedness.


Nyn is the space where everything is possible, where chaos becomes clarity, stress becomes calmness and ideas and creativity flow freely.
